# Useful Language: Module 6 — "Self-Reflecting and Analyzing Your Work"

---

## Evaluating Your Progress and Setting Objectives

### Setting Objectives
- One thing I'm going to do is …
- I intend to …
- I'd like to focus on …

### Commenting on Progress
- I'm really happy with …
- I've put a lot of effort in …
- I'm making progress in / with …
- *[Over the last month]* I've been working with [someone] / on [project]
- I feel I did a great job *[leading the team]*
- I struggled to …

### Talking About Things to Improve
- Something I need to work on is …
- I think … is an area for improvement.

---

## Talking About Your Performance

- become better at something
- a sense of achievement
- room for improvement
- be involved in something
- benefit from something
- expand my *[communication/organizational]* skills/knowledge
- try my hand at something

---

## Giving Constructive Feedback

### Saying What Went Well
- I thought it was very helpful to …
- It was really nice to see how …

### Saying What Didn't Go Well
- I wish I'd/we'd [3rd form of verb]
- I had a hard time [verb+*ing*] because …
- because/as/since [subject+verb]
- because of [noun]
- So, / As a result, [subject+verb]

### Suggesting Improvements
- I think it would be very helpful if we could …
- It might be better to …
- We've got to make sure that …

---

## Having a Productive Discussion

### Taking and Keeping Your Turn in Discussions
- Sorry to interrupt.
- Sorry to butt in. *(informal)*
- Can I just say something here?
- Sorry, just one more thing.

### Stopping an Unproductive Discussion
- I suppose that's a little off topic.
- Can we park that (until the end of the meeting) and first look at …?

### Dealing with Misunderstanding
- Sorry, that's not what I mean.
- Sorry if I didn't make that clear.
