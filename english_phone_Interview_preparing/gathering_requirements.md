## Avoiding Misunderstanding When Gathering Requirements

### Asking for Clarification
- What do you mean by …?
- Could you elaborate on …?

### Checking Understanding by Rephrasing
- So basically what you're saying is … Is that correct/right?
- If I understand you correctly, …

### Asking for Repetition
- Sorry, I didn't catch *[that]*. Could you say *[the last bit]* again?

### Building Rapport and Showing Respect
- Thank you for clearing that up. *(less formal)*
- Thank you for the clarification. *(more formal)*
- Let me explain why I'm asking this.

### Summarizing
- To make sure we're on the same page, let me sum up what we've *[discussed / talked about / agreed on]*.
- So, (just) to recap, …

## Asking Questions When Gathering Requirements

### Asking About the Problem / Situation
- Could you briefly explain to me what the problem is?
- Can you tell me why it was decided to implement the changes?
- What do you think is the likely cause of the problem?
- What is the primary goal of the project and how would you define its success?
- Can you provide me with an overview of the system?

### Asking About the People Involved
- Who should I contact for more specific questions about the architecture?
- Who will be using the dashboard?

### Asking About Time
- When is the report due?
- What sort of time frame do you have in mind?
- Is there a deadline for this model?

### Asking About the Result of Analysis
- Do you have any preferences about how the results will be presented?
- What metrics are we going to focus on?
- Are there any other hypotheses you'd like to test?
- What format would you like the results in?

## Answering Technical Interview Questions

### Buy Time to Think or Reformulate the Question
- So, you're asking me …
- Let me think about this for a second.
- Well, off the top of my head, I'd say that …
- That's a(n) *[good/tough/interesting]* question.

### Make a Point
- I'd say that …
- As I see it, …
- From my point of view, …
- Well, the thing is, …

### Give an Example
- For instance, …
- Just to give you an example, …

### Reformulate Your Point
- In a nutshell, …
- To sum up, …
- So, to recap, …

## Describing a Task

### Outlining the Current Situation
- Just to give you a bit of background (on the situation), …
- The problem (we're facing here) is that …
- The situation (as it stands) is that …

### Explaining the Solution/Task and Its Purpose
- What we need is … (so that we can …)
- What's important is *[that these events should have certain parameters]*.
- This will allow us / enable us to …

### Making a Request
- Is that something you can help with?
- Would you be able to …?

### Asking About Difficulties/Time
- How long do you think it will take?
- Do you see any blockers (to that)?

## Linking Ideas

### Showing Reason
- as / since / because
- because of [noun / pronoun]

### Showing Result
- … which means (that …) / That means (that …)
- … which could lead to [noun] / That could lead to [noun]
- … which may result in [noun] / That may result in [noun]

### Showing Purpose
- so that [subject + (can/will) + verb]

## Common Questions & Answers

*   When is the presentation due?
    *   *Ideally, the end of next week. Would that be realistic?*
*   When exactly were these changes implemented?
    *   *At the beginning of June, about three weeks ago.*
*   What is the primary goal of the campaign?
    *   *Well, it's aimed at getting new free users to the service.*
*   Who should I contact for more specific questions about the architecture?
*   How should the results be presented?
    *   *I guess an Excel report will do.*
*   Who can I contact if I need more information?
    *   *Please reach out to Jarek Nowak, the data owner, and Boris, our financial expert.*
*   Are there any other hypotheses we need to test?
    *   *Yes. It's quite likely that users are having difficulty accessing the feature.*
*   What do you think is the likely cause of the problem?
    *   *I think it might be related to poor user experience or navigation.*
*   Who will be using the results of the analysis?
    *   *The finance and the marketing teams.*
*   What metrics are we going to focus on?
    *   *I think we need to consider new customer growth rate, churn, and active users.*

## Dialog Example — Requirements Gathering

> **Vlad:** OK, I guess we are good to go. I have scheduled this meeting to get a better understanding of what is required from the data team. I have seen the overview you sent me, but it'd be great if you could clarify some information. Can you briefly explain what the primary goal of the campaign is?
>
> **Petra:** Our app user base is not as large as we'd like, so I'd say the main goal of the program is to quickly increase the number of active clients by attracting more users in the short run.
>
> **Vlad:** What do you mean by "in the short run"?
>
> **Petra:** Well, our short-term goal is to increase the number of active users by at least 10% by the end of Q4.
>
> **Vlad:** Got it. I've got a question about the recent user data. Is there an opportunity to see what kind of code is activated when a user signs up? We'll need to separate referral codes from the other promo codes available to our clients. And when was the program launched?
>
> **Petra:** A week ago, on August sixth. In our architecture the referral codes are supposed to be different from other codes — we tried to keep that in mind when launching the program. I'll ask the development team how to figure out the difference and get back to you.
>
> **Vlad:** Sorry, I didn't catch that. Can you say the last bit again?
>
> **Petra:** I'll reach out to the developers and find that out for you.
>
> **Vlad:** Sounds good, thanks. Do you think it would be possible to wait for at least a few more weeks and collect more data before jumping to conclusions about the effectiveness of the program? The thing is, there could be seasonality in data due to external reasons like holidays or weather changes, so it'd be reasonable to collect a larger sample to get a clearer picture and detect any outliers.
>
> **Petra:** Well, we do realize that this is an initial evaluation, and it'd be easier for your team to draw some conclusions when there's more data.
>
> **Vlad:** If I understand you correctly, you'd like us to carry out the analysis using the data we've collected over the last week. Is that right?
>
> **Petra:** That's right. It's really important for us to get this initial evaluation as soon as possible.
>
> **Vlad:** OK. I've got a few more questions if you don't mind. What metrics are we going to focus on?
>
> **Petra:** I guess it'd be great to see the absolute number of referred users and what percentage they make up of total new users so we can understand how attractive the campaign is. But it's also essential to calculate our PnL and check that we're not losing money on referrals.
>
> **Vlad:** Is there a deadline for preparing the report?
>
> **Petra:** I was hoping we could have the report by the end of the week. Does that sound realistic?
>
> **Vlad:** I think so. It shouldn't take too long. OK, to make sure we're on the same page, let me sum up what we've discussed so far. We seem to have all the data available, and we're not going to account for seasonality. The metrics we're going to be looking at are absolute and relative increase in new users, PnL, and revenue per referred client. Does that sound about right?
>
> **Petra:** I think so, yes.
>
> **Vlad:** Good. Just a couple more questions.

## Case Study — Digital Twin

You're an analyst at an industrial company. The head of sales wants to introduce a "digital twin" of the organization which will help to oversee and manage all the processes related to the company's sales operations. Basically, they want a solution (probably a dashboard or something similar) that needs to be two-way integrated with the ERP (enterprise resource planning) system. This tool should allow them to get Slack notifications so that they can see what's happening with orders and adjust system data within the dashboard.

You've been asked to implement this "digital twin". You will be meeting with the head of sales to gather some initial requirements and find out what your management wants.

*What questions would you prepare for the meeting? What would you ask the stakeholder about?*

## Describing a Task — Case Study

You're an analyst at an e-commerce company. Your team is building a global dashboard that has to work for all the countries where your company operates. The dashboard will display lots of data, among which is information about client interactions. Currently, this information is stored differently across regions, and using various source systems and databases isn't convenient. The dashboard's back end is not optimal for the data modelling that is necessary for a global solution, so it was decided to design a multi-layer data structure. You've created a data model for client interactions for one country and would like to scale its logic to the databases for the other regions. In order to do that, you need help from a data engineer.

You've scheduled a call so that you can explain the task to them. Here's some additional information about the situation that you may need:

*   **Task:** You need to synchronize the output data format across countries/markets. You've created a table for Japan and would like it to be used as an example for the tables used in the other countries.
*   **Why it's important:** The insights from the dashboard will be used by all the senior managers across the whole company. They want to have the ability to compare key characteristics between countries.
*   **Details of the task:** Data quality checks and business rules may be region-specific. It's important to check that data meets local standards with the help of automatic or user-based checks as well as system rules and documentation.

## Dialog Examples

### Report Automation Delay

> **Analyst:** … All right, let's get started. I scheduled this call to talk about possible improvements to the automated process of sending the internal sales reports. As you know, our internal system is set up in such a way that it sends daily sales and marketing reports to certain Slack channels at a fixed time. The situation, as is stands, is that sometimes these reports are based on figures that seem to be outdated. This could be because of some delays in updating the data marts. Let's say, they got updated at 9.02, but the sales report was sent out at 9 o'clock sharp. Unfortunately, such situations aren't uncommon. The problem is that stakeholders don't get the most recent information, which means they sometimes have to put off making some decisions. And this has a negative effect on the decision-making process. So, what we need is to build a pipeline when the reports are sent after the data sources get updated. Would you be able to help with that?
>
> **Engineer:** If I understand you correctly, you don't want to send the reports unless the tables contain the recent data. Is that what you're saying?
>
> **Analyst:** Yes, pretty much. I actually see two options here. The first is having the system send the reports at a fixed time, but what's important is that it happens only after the data source has been updated. Alternatively, reports don't have a scheduled time and are sent right after the successful update. Oh, one more thing — we also need the system to generate a message in the analyst's channel notifying our team of the delays. It'll allow us to be up to speed on what's happening and deal with stakeholders' questions about the reports much faster.
>
> **Engineer:** Ok, got it. I'll see what I can do.
>
> **Analyst:** Perfect, thanks. How long do you think this will take?
>
> **Engineer:** Well, it shouldn't take me long, but I'm not sure I have time for it this week. I could try to fit it into next week's sprint. You might want to come to our planning meeting on Friday so that we can discuss the priority of this task.

### Eliminating Duplicates

> *Exploring a user's journey takes longer than expected because of them, and it really complicates our work.*
> *That could also lead to longer processing time and extra costs. So we need something to eliminate those duplicates at the build stage so that we won't have to delete them manually.*
>
> **Engineer:** But I'm not sure if it's the best thing to do since it's a huge amount of data that you might never need. Perhaps it's easier if you deal with the duplicates in your queries.
>
> **Analyst:** I see what you mean. But we currently have several reports based on that data, which means that one day of data may get a hundred of queries. So it's a good idea to clean it at the initial stage so that we can use unique data later on.

## Results Presentation Tips

### Tip 1. Introduction
You might want to skip the introductory part if it's a one-on-one meeting with a stakeholder as they already know the background for the business problem. In other cases, consider describing the context, addressing the business problem, and reminding the stakeholder about the prior agreements or discussions.

**Useful language:**
*   Having analyzed *[this data]*, I've come up with …
*   Let me walk you through …

### Tip 2. Methodology
Make sure you go over the approach you took when carrying out the analysis. Find the right level of information that is not too simple for the more technical people and is not too complex for those who are less technical. Start with general findings, giving an overview of the available data and then diving into it.

### Tip 3. Visualization
Share only the key metrics that you want the audience to focus on. Refer to slides or graphics to give a consistent and visual approach to your speech.

### Tip 4. Conclusion and Recommendations
Make sure that your research provides clear insights; if needed, add some recommendations for the business.

**Making conclusions (less certain):**
*   We might expect that …
*   These *[scenarios/data]* suggest that …
*   It appears that …

**Making conclusions (more certain):**
*   It's safe to *[say/conclude]* that …
*   This is a strong indicator that …

**Giving recommendations:**
*   It would probably make sense to …
*   It's probably worth [verb + *ing*] …
*   It might be reasonable to …
