### Question 23

#### Question
**Addressing a Team Conflict** — Read the task carefully and write your response in 150–200 words.
You are working on a project with colleagues from different departments. One team member is not meeting deadlines, which affects the whole project.
Write a message to this colleague:
- describe the problem and its impact on the team
- suggest a way to improve the situation
- keep the tone polite and constructive

#### Answer
> Hi Alex,
>
> I wanted to talk about the deadlines on our cross-department project. Over the last few sprints, several of your tasks have slipped past their due dates, and because other teams depend on them, the whole project has fallen behind.
>
> Looking back, I think part of the problem is how we plan. We make our estimates in live planning sessions, and I've noticed you rarely get a chance to speak up there, so the estimates for your tasks may not reflect how much work they really involve. That makes our schedule unreliable for everyone.
>
> I'd like to suggest a change: let's try asynchronous planning in Slack. A bot would collect everyone's estimates in writing before the sprint starts, so each of us has time to think and can raise concerns without being put on the spot. If something still looks unrealistic, we can adjust the scope together early instead of missing the deadline later.
>
> Could we have a quick chat this week to try it out? I'm sure we can get things back on track together.
>
> Best regards,
> Aleksandr
