# Productive meetings

[How to Interrupt Someone in a Nice Way](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/how-to-interrupt-someone-in-a-nice-way/4735900.html)

Several examples of phrases and strategies for

**politely interjecting in meetings**

. Here are some key examples:

1. **Asking for Permission**:
    - "Do you mind if I jump in?"
    - "Excuse me, may I add something?"
2. **Acknowledging Contributions**:
    - "I appreciate your insights, but I’d like to add..."
    - "Before we move on, let me add..."
3. **Seeking Clarification**:
    - "Forgive me, but I’m a little confused about..."
    - "Could you please recap that for me?"
4. **Disagreeing Politely**:
    - "I respectfully disagree with this assessment..."
    - "To play devil’s advocate, what if we considered...?"
5. **Transitioning the Conversation**:
    - "Can we save this for another meeting? We have other aspects to cover today."
    - "I think this is a great topic to discuss further, but let’s focus on our agenda for now."
