# Phrasal Verbs

*   **Give up** – Stop doing something; surrender
    *   *She gave up smoking last year.*

*   **Go over** – Review or examine carefully
    *   *Let’s go over the report one more time.*

*   **Hold on** – Wait for a short time
    *   *Hold on, I’ll be right there.*

*   **Keep up (with)** – Stay at the same level or pace
    *   *He runs so fast, I can't keep up with him.*

*   **Look after** – Take care of someone/something
    *   *Can you look after my dog while I'm away?*

*   **Look into** – Investigate or research
    *   *The police are looking into the matter.*

*   **Make up (for something)** – Compensate
    *   *I'll make up for being late by working overtime.*

*   **Make up** – Invent (a story, lie, excuse) or reconcile
    *   *He made up an excuse for missing the meeting.*
    *   *They argued but later made up.*

*   **Pass out** – Faint or lose consciousness
    *   *He passed out from the heat.*

*   **Pick up** – Collect someone/something or learn casually
    *   *Can you pick me up from the airport?*
    *   *She picked up some Spanish while traveling.*

*   **Put off** – Postpone or discourage
    *   *Let’s put off the meeting until next week.*
    *   *The smell put me off my food.*

*   **Run into** – Meet unexpectedly
    *   *I ran into an old friend at the mall.*

*   **Set up** – Arrange or establish
    *   *They set up a new company in Berlin.*

*   **Show up** – Arrive or appear
    *   *He didn’t show up for the appointment.*

*   **Take after** – Resemble (usually family)
    *   *She takes after her mother.*

*   **Take off** – Become successful (for trends); remove (clothes); leave the ground (planes)
    *   *The startup really took off last year.*
    *   *He took off his coat.*
    *   *The plane took off on time.*

*   **Turn down** – Reject
    *   *She turned down the job offer.*

*   **Work out** – Exercise or resolve
    *   *I work out three times a week.*
    *   *It all worked out in the end.*
