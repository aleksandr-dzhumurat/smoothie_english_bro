# Useful Language: Module 1 — "Self-Presentation"

---

## Doing Small Talk

### Greeting People and Replying to Greetings
- Hi (name), how are you? — Not bad, thanks. How about you?
- Hi (name), how are you doing? — Doing good, thank you.
- How's it going? — Fine/Well, thank you.

### Beginning Small Talk
- What's the weather like in (city)?
- How's the weather (where you are/over there)?
- How's your (onboarding/project/day) going?
- What time is it there?
- How's your (first working week/day) been so far?
- Are you working from (home/the office)?
- Where are you based?
- Have you ever been to (city/country)?

### Giving Short Comments
- Great! / Oh, I see / Yeah / Right.
- Happy to hear that.
- What a pain/bummer.

### Giving Longer Comments
- I'd love to travel there one day, it's on my list.
- We're (three hours) ahead of you.

### 'So, Tell Me More' (Follow-Up Questions)
- How long have you lived there?

### Ending Small Talk
- Let's get started, shall we?
- Let's kick off.

## Building Rapport

### Finding Common Ground
- Are you keen on (verb+*ing*)?
- How did you get into (verb+*ing*)?
- We're having a/an (name of event). Are you gonna come?
- Whereabouts in (Zurich) do you live?
- [Are you] going anywhere nice?
- What are you up to this weekend?

### Talking About Interests
- I'm (not) really into it.
- I'm fond of (verb+*ing*).
- It's a great stress-buster.
- I'm a bit of an opera fan.
- I enjoy going out.

### Talking About Plans
- I haven't got anything booked.
- I'm taking a few days off.

## Giving a Self-Presentation

### Talking About Your Role and Responsibilities
- I'm a (position) with (more than/over) … years of professional experience.
- I'm currently a (position) at (company) where I …

### Talking About Your Background and Work Experience
- I started out as a (position) working at (company).
- My background is in (area).
- I've always had an interest in …
- Previously, I worked at (company).

### Talking About Your Skills and Knowledge
- I have a(n) (extensive/good/thorough/deep) knowledge of …
- I'm (also) proficient in (something/verb+*ing*).

### Saying How You Feel About the Job
- I think my skills and experience make me a good fit for the job.

## Using the STAR Framework to Talk About Past Experiences

### Situation
- On my previous project …
- In my last job, I worked with …
- When I was working on the ABC project, …

### Task
- The customer wanted us to …
- I had to … / The task was to …

### Action
- I spent a few days (collecting data).
- I developed …
- So, what I did was I (investigated the problem).

### Result
- As a result, … / So, …
- I learned a lot from the experience.
