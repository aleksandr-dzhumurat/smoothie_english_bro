# Unit 73 — the 1

# Theory

## A the when there is only one

If there is only one of something in the context, we use **the**:

- Have you ever flown over the North Pole? (there is only one)
- Our office is on the ninth floor.
- Lima is the capital of Peru.
- I'll pay you back at the end of the week.

**the** is also used with **superlatives**: *the fastest train*, *the most expensive seat*.

Compare **the** (only one) and **a/an** (one of many / what kind):

- The moon is a satellite. (= one of many satellites)
- The flat we rented was a very modern flat.
- What's the quickest way to learn a new alphabet?

## B the same

Always **the same**: *Your jacket is the same colour as mine.* ❌ *is same colour* / *"Are these two keys the same?" "No, they're different."*

## C the world, the sky, the sea …

We say: **the world, the universe, the sun, the moon, the earth, the sky, the sea, the ground, the country** (= countryside).

- I love watching the stars in the sky. ❌ *in sky*
- Do you prefer living in a city or in the country?
- The moon orbits the earth.

Two exceptions:

- **Earth** (no *the*) when we think of it as a planet among others: *Venus is the planet closest to Earth.*
- **space** (no *the*) = outer space: *There is no sound in space.* ❌ *in the space*. But *the space* = a gap/area: *I couldn't fit the car into the space.*

## D the cinema, the radio … but TV

| with the | without the |
|---|---|
| go to the cinema, go to the theatre | watch TV / watch television |
| listen to the radio | — |
| the internet | — |

*I go to the theatre a lot but I rarely watch TV.* Note: **the television** = the TV set: *Could you turn the television down?*

## E Meals

Meals are normally used **without the**: *What time is lunch?* / *We had dinner at a small trattoria.*

But with an adjective we use **a/an**: *a big breakfast*, *a lovely dinner*, *a late lunch*. ❌ *We had lovely dinner.*

## F Numbers and labels

No **the** before a noun + number/letter used as a label: **platform 4, room 302, page 61, size 38, vitamin D, section C, gate 12**.

- The Berlin train leaves from platform 4. ❌ *the platform 4*
- Do you have these trainers in size 38?

**See also:** a/an → Unit 71; a/an and the → Unit 72; the 2–4 → Units 74–76; Names with and without the → Units 77–78.

### Question 46

#### Question
Fill the gaps: "Mercury is ___ planet nearest to ___ sun, and it's also ___ smallest planet in ___ solar system."

#### Answer
The correct sentence is *Mercury is the planet nearest to the sun, and it's also the smallest planet in the solar system.* **the planet nearest to** identifies one specific planet; **the sun** is unique in our context; **the smallest** is a superlative, which always takes **the**; and there is only one **solar system** we normally talk about. Using *a* with a superlative (*a smallest planet*) is impossible, and *sun* without an article is not possible for a singular countable noun.

### Question 50

#### Question
An airport announcement reads: "Flight ZK402 to Oslo is now boarding at ___." Which option is right?

#### Answer
The correct completion is *gate 17*. When a noun is combined with a number or letter as a label — *gate 17, platform 2, room 105, page 30, size 42, vitamin B* — we do not use **the**. *The gate 17* is a very common learner error; *a gate 17* is wrong because the number already identifies exactly one gate; *gate the 17* is not an English pattern. Note that *the gate* without a number would be fine (*Please close the gate*), but not with the number.
