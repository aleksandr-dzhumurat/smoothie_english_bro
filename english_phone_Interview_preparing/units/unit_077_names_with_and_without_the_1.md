# Unit 77 — Names with and without the 1

# Theory

## A People and most place names → no the

Names of people (*Marta*, *Marta Kowalska*) take no article, and neither do **most** place names:

| type | examples (no the) |
|---|---|
| continents | Asia, Europe, North America |
| countries, states, provinces | Portugal, Vietnam, California, Bavaria |
| islands | Crete, Madagascar |
| cities, towns, villages | Lisbon, Osaka, Grantchester |
| single mountains | Mont Blanc, Fuji, Denali |

❌ *the Portugal*, ❌ *the Asia*

**But** names containing a common noun such as *Republic, Kingdom, States, Federation, Emirates* usually take **the**: *the Czech Republic, the United Kingdom, the United States, the Russian Federation, the United Arab Emirates*.

- We flew from Ireland to the United States.

## B Titles + names; Mount and Lake

A **title + name** takes no article: *Mrs Okafor, Doctor Alvarez, Captain Reyes, President Lincoln, Professor Ng, Uncle Sami, Saint Nicholas, Queen Victoria*. ❌ *the Doctor Alvarez*

Compare: *We phoned the doctor.* (a doctor, no name) vs *We phoned Doctor Alvarez.*

**Mount** and **Lake** + name work the same way: *Mount Fuji, Mount Vesuvius, Lake Geneva, Lake Titicaca*. ❌ *the Lake Geneva*

- Our cabin is beside the lake. / Our cabin is beside Lake Geneva.

## C Water and deserts → the

**Oceans, seas, rivers, canals, deserts** take **the**:

| oceans/seas | rivers/canals | deserts |
|---|---|---|
| the Pacific (Ocean), the Baltic (Sea), the Caribbean | the Danube, the Thames, the Panama Canal | the Kalahari (Desert), the Atacama |

- The Danube flows through ten countries.

## D Plural names → the

Names that are **plural** take **the**, whether people or places:

| type | examples |
|---|---|
| families | the Kowalskis, the Simpsons (= the Simpson family) |
| plural countries | the Netherlands, the Philippines, the Maldives |
| island groups | the Azores, the Hebrides, the Galápagos (Islands) |
| mountain ranges | the Pyrenees, the Rockies, the Himalayas |

- The highest peak in the Pyrenees is Aneto.

## E Compass points

**the north / the south / the west (of …)** with *the*; **northern / southern / western** as adjectives without *the*:

- Porto is in the north of Portugal. = Porto is in northern Portugal.
- Norway is in northern Europe; Greece is in the south.

In many **names** of regions and countries, north/south etc. appear without *the*: *North Korea, South Australia, West Africa, southeast Asia*. On maps *the* is usually omitted from all names.

### Question 39

#### Question
Spot the mistake: "When I was ill in Madrid, the Doctor Ibáñez told me to stay in bed, and the hotel receptionist called the doctor again the next day."

#### Answer
The mistake is *the Doctor Ibáñez*. When a title (*Doctor, Professor, Captain, President, Mr*) is followed by a **name**, no article is used: *Doctor Ibáñez*. The second use, *called the doctor*, is correct because there is no name — *the doctor* simply means "the doctor we had been dealing with". So the contrast is: *the doctor* (title alone, article possible) vs *Doctor Ibáñez* (title + name, no article).

#### Correct variant
¯\_(ツ)_/¯

#### Variants
1. *the doctor again* should be *doctor again*, because we already know which doctor.
2. *the Doctor Ibáñez* should be *Doctor Ibáñez*: title + name takes no article. (Correct)
3. *in Madrid* should be *in the Madrid*.
4. Both *the Doctor Ibáñez* and *the doctor* are correct; there is no mistake.

### Question 41

#### Question
Which sentence correctly names a mountain range, an ocean and a desert?

#### Answer
The correct sentence is *The Andes, the Pacific and the Atacama Desert are all in South America.* Mountain **ranges** are plural names and take **the** (*the Andes*, unlike a single mountain such as *Aconcagua*); **oceans** take **the** (*the Pacific*); **deserts** take **the** (*the Atacama Desert*). *South America* is a continent name and takes no article. The other options each drop *the* from one of the three geographical names that require it.

### Question 42

#### Question
Two sentences describe where a city is. Which pair is grammatically correct?

#### Answer
The correct pair is *Bergen is in the west of Norway.* / *Bergen is in western Norway.* With a compass **noun** (*north, south, west*) we say **the west of** a country; with the **adjective** (*northern, southern, western*) there is no article: *western Norway*. Option 1 drops *the* before the noun *west*; option 2 wrongly adds *the* before the adjective *western*; option 4 makes both errors. Note that region **names** like *North Africa* or *southeast Asia* are exceptions used without *the*.

#### Correct variant
¯\_(ツ)_/¯

#### Variants
1. Bergen is in west of Norway. / Bergen is in western Norway.
2. Bergen is in the west of Norway. / Bergen is in the western Norway.
3. Bergen is in the west of Norway. / Bergen is in western Norway. (Correct)
4. Bergen is in west of Norway. / Bergen is in the western Norway.
