# Unit 1 — Present continuous (I am doing)

# Theory

## A Form

The present continuous is **am/is/are + -ing**. It describes an action that is going on now, at the moment of speaking, and is not finished yet. Picture Daniel on a train with his laptop open: *He's working on a report.* The work is in progress right now.

| Subject | am/is/are | -ing form |
|---|---|---|
| I | am (I'm) | writing |
| he / she / it | is (he's, she's, it's) | cooking |
| we / you / they | are (we're, you're, they're) | waiting |

Negative: *I'm not listening. She isn't sleeping. They aren't coming.*
Question: *Are you listening? Is it snowing? What are they building?*

## B Meaning: in the middle of an action

**I am doing something** = I have started it and I have not finished; I am in the middle of it.

- Turn the music down, please. *I'm trying* to concentrate. (not I try)
- 'Where's Olga?' '*She's making* a phone call.' (not She makes)
- We can leave the umbrella. *It isn't raining* now. (not It doesn't rain)
- How's the new flat? *Are you settling* in?
- Why is everyone shouting? *What's going on?*

The action does not have to be happening at the exact second you speak. It is enough that you are in the middle of a longer process:

- *I'm reading* a biography of Napoleon at the moment. (I started it, I haven't finished it — but I may not be holding the book right now.)
- Marta wants to move to Lisbon, so *she's learning* Portuguese.
- My neighbours *are renovating* their kitchen. They hope to finish before Christmas.

## C Periods around now

The present continuous also works with **today / this week / this month / this year** — time periods that surround the present:

- *You're spending* a lot of time at the gym this week. (not You spend)
- Our sales team *isn't performing* very well this year.

## D Changes and developments

Use the present continuous for a change that has started and is still in progress. Typical verbs: **get, become, change, improve, start, begin, increase, rise, fall, grow**.

- *Is* your back *getting* better? (not Does your back get better)
- House prices in the city *are rising* fast. (not rise)
- I hated running at first, but *I'm beginning* to enjoy it. (not I begin)

❌ *The weather changes, look at the sky.* ✅ *The weather is changing, look at the sky.*
