# Unit 127 — in/on/at (other uses)

# Theory

## A in — weather, languages, feelings, opinions

| pattern | examples |
|---|---|
| in + weather/light | in the rain, in the sun, in the shade, in the dark, in the fog, in bad weather |
| in + language / currency / unit | in Spanish, in euros, in miles |
| (be / fall) in love (with sb) | *They fell in love on a school trip.* |
| in a (good/bad) mood | *He's in a terrible mood this morning.* |
| in (my) opinion | *In my opinion the sequel is better than the original.* |

- *The dog refused to go out in the rain.*
- *Let's sit in the shade; it's too hot in the sun.*
- *How do you say "cheers" in Portuguese?* / *What's that in euros?*

## B on — media, phone, fire, purpose, the whole

| expression | example |
|---|---|
| on TV / on television | *Is the match on TV tonight?* |
| on the radio | *I heard the interview on the radio.* |
| on the phone | *She spends hours on the phone.* |
| on fire | *The shed is on fire! Call 999.* |
| on purpose (= intentionally) | *He broke it on purpose, not by accident.* |
| on the whole (= in general) | *On the whole, the feedback was positive.* |

## C on holiday / on a trip / on business etc.

- (be/go) **on holiday / on vacation**: *We're going on holiday in August.*
- (be/go) **on a trip / on a tour / on a cruise / on an excursion**: *They're on a cruise round the fjords.*
- (be / go to a place) **on business**: *My manager is in Berlin on business.*
- (be/go) **on strike**: *The nurses are on strike this week.*
- (be/go) **on a diet**: *I've gone on a diet before the wedding.*

Note: *go somewhere **for** a holiday* is also possible: *They went to Crete for a holiday.*

## D at — age, speed, temperature, altitude

**at** + measured values:
- **at the age of 18 / at 18**: *She left home at 18* or *at the age of 18*.
- **at 70 miles an hour / at a speed of …**: *The car was doing at least 90 mph* → *travelling at 90 miles an hour*.
- **at 0 degrees / at 100 degrees**: *Water freezes at 0 °C.*
- **at an altitude / a height of …**: *We were cruising at an altitude of 10,000 metres.*

**See also:** in/at/on (time) → Unit 121; in/at/on (position) → Units 123–125.

### Question 44

#### Question
One of these sentences is wrong. Which, and how should it be fixed?

#### Answer
**"My grandfather retired in the age of 65" is wrong; it should be "at the age of 65" (or simply "at 65").** Ages, speeds, temperatures and altitudes take *at*: *at 65, at 120 km/h, at 100 degrees, at an altitude of 5,000 m*. The other three sentences are correct.

#### Correct variant
¯\_(ツ)_/¯

#### Variants
1. "Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius" — should be *in 100 degrees*.
2. "My grandfather retired in the age of 65" — should be *at the age of 65*. (Correct)
3. "The plane was flying at 900 km an hour" — should be *with 900 km an hour*.
4. "She learned to swim at six" — should be *on six*.

### Question 45

#### Question
A witness says: "The car wasn't ___ fire when I got there. But the driver hit the lamppost ___ purpose — he did it deliberately. ___ the whole, though, nobody was hurt." Fill the gaps.

#### Answer
**on fire … on purpose … On the whole.** All three are fixed *on* expressions: *on fire* (burning), *on purpose* (intentionally, the opposite of *by accident*), *on the whole* (in general). *In fire*, *by purpose* and *at the whole* do not exist; *by accident* is the correct form for the opposite meaning, which is why *by purpose* is a tempting but wrong distractor.

#### Correct variant
¯\_(ツ)_/¯

#### Variants
1. in fire … by purpose … In the whole
2. on fire … by purpose … At the whole
3. at fire … on purpose … In the whole
4. on fire … on purpose … On the whole (Correct)
