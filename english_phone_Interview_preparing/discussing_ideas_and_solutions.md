# Useful Language: Module 5 — "Discussing Ideas and Solutions"

---

## Suggesting Solutions and Giving Arguments

### Acknowledging Another Point of View
- I see what you mean.
- I see what you're saying.

### Making a Suggestion
- I would suggest [verb+*ing*]
- What we could do is [verb]

### Mentioning an Advantage or Disadvantage
- One really (good/great) thing about … is that …
- (It'll allow you to …) And what this gives (you/us) is …
- And not only that, …
- The downside (of …) is that …
- One of the limitations (of …) is that …

### Expressing Certainty
- I'm sure that you will see the benefits of *[this]*.
- … is a tried and tested *[practice/technique/method/approach]*.

## Writing a Meeting Follow-Up

### Greeting
- Dear [name], / Dear all, *(more formal)*
- Hello/Hi [name],
- Hello/Hi everyone,

### Showing Appreciation
- Thank you for taking the time to *[meet and discuss the …]*
- Thank you for attending the *[demo]*.
- Thanks for the meeting. *(less formal)*

### Summarizing and Outlining Action Points
- Here's a (quick) recap of what we talked about.
- Below is a summary of the action items and next steps.
- Following up on our conversation, I'd like to sum up the key points discussed today. *(more formal)*

### Mentioning Extra Materials
- I've attached *[the file you asked for]*.
- Here is a link to *[the meeting recording]*.

### Closing
- (Please) feel free to reach out with any questions or concerns.
- (Please) let me know if you have any questions or there is anything I've missed.

### Signing Off
- Best / Kind regards,
- Best, *(less formal)*
- Looking forward to talking to you *[next Monday]*.

## Participating in a Discussion

### Giving an Opinion
- I tend to think …
- If you ask me …

### Asking for an Opinion
- What are your thoughts on this?
- What's your take on this?

### Interrupting and Reacting to Interruptions
- Can I jump in here?
- Sure, go ahead. / Could I just finish my point?

### Reacting to Someone's Opinion: Agreeing and Disagreeing
- I couldn't agree more.
- Absolutely. / Exactly.
- That's a good/valid point.
- You have a point there, but …

## Answering a Critical Thinking Skills Question

### Starting the Story
- I must admit *[I haven't had a lot of situations like that]*.
- I'm not so sure about that.
- I would disagree (with that).

### Suggesting Ideas
- What if we …?
- Why don't we …?

### Describing Solutions
- increase the engagement rate
- quick fix solution
- settle on *[a solution/decision/strategy]*
- roll out *[a program/feature/service]*
- low-cost solution
- pay off
- meet the *[terms/needs/requirements]* of
- run the risk of

### Focusing on Your Actions
- I highlighted (that …)
- I pointed out (that …)

---

## Demonstrating Problem-Solving Skills at an Interview

- get on with *[a task]*
- address *[a problem / an issue]*
- dissatisfied with something
- figure something out
- miss the deadline
- experience delays
- handle *[a situation / a case]*
- What we did was we *[tested both approaches]*.

### Sequencing Actions
- While/before/after [verb+*ing*] …, I [past form] …
  - *Before choosing one solution, we tested both of them.*
  - *While doing the experiment, I got some unexpected results.*

### Mentioning Details
- Through my analysis, it became clear (that …)
- As it turned out …

### Signaling the End
- (I think) that's basically it.
